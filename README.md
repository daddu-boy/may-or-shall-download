# May or Shall for Windows and Mac

Installers for the May or Shall desktop app. Select text in any app, Outlook, WhatsApp, Word or a PDF reader, and the May or Shall icon appears beside it; click it to save the line to your matter.

**Download:** [app.mayorshall.com/download](https://app.mayorshall.com/download)

- Mac with Apple silicon: `May-or-Shall-mac-arm64.dmg`
- Intel Mac: `May-or-Shall-mac-x64.dmg`
- Windows 10 or 11: `May-or-Shall-win-x64.exe`

The first time it opens, Windows or macOS shows a notice, because the app is new and not yet signed by Microsoft or Apple. On Windows click More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac open System Settings, then Privacy and Security, and click Open Anyway. On a Mac, allow Accessibility when asked, so the icon can appear in other apps.

The app reads a selection only when you make one, and only to save it when you click the icon. See the [privacy policy](https://app.mayorshall.com/privacy).
